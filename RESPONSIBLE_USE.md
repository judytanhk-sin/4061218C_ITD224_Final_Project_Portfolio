# Responsible Use, Ethics and Limitations

## Intended Use

This project is intended for academic analysis of historical HDB resale
transactions and demonstration of the CRISP-DM data science workflow.

## Limitations

Model predictions are based on historical transaction data and selected
property features. They may not reflect current market conditions,
renovation quality, exact location, nearby amenities, buyer preferences
or future government policies.

## Responsible Use

Predicted prices should be treated as reference estimates rather than
official property valuations or financial advice. Buyers and sellers
should consult qualified property professionals and official sources
before making financial decisions.

## Privacy and Data Ethics

The project uses publicly available transaction data and does not attempt
to identify individual buyers or sellers.

## Fairness

Model outputs should not be used to discriminate against individuals or
communities. Differences between towns should be interpreted carefully
and in the context of property attributes and market conditions.
